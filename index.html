About This Project

The East Africa Snakes Guide is an educational project designed to provide information about at least 20 snake species found in East Africa. It explores their physical characteristics, habitats, distribution, feeding habits and conservation importance.

Snake Conservation and Safety

Snakes play important roles in maintaining ecological balance, including controlling rodent populations. Understanding and protecting them contributes to biodiversity conservation.
